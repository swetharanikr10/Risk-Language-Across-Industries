# Risk-Language-Across-Industries
An NLP-Based Analysis of Risk Disclosures Across Industries (2021–2025)

NLP analysis in R of Item 1A – Risk Factors from 30 SEC 10-K filings: JPM, BAC, GS (banking), AAPL, MSFT (tech) and JNJ (pharma), fiscal years 2021–2025.

Methods: word frequency, TF-IDF, cosine similarity, hierarchical clustering, LDA (k = 4), Loughran-McDonald sentiment, keyword trends.

Key Findings

Company outweighs industry: mean similarity is 0.90 within a company, 0.41 within an industry and 0.23 across industries.
J&J resembles Apple, not the banks: both describe manufacturing and supply-chain risk.
AI mentions grew more than 7× (0.46 to 3.50 per 1,000 tokens) and cybersecurity mentions more than doubled. COVID mentions nearly disappeared.
Banks use the most negative language; J&J uses the most litigious language.
